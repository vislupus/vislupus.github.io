The Seven Rila Lakes are the most visited place in the Rila mountains, and for a good reason: in a few hours the path climbs past seven glacial lakes, each on its own terrace. This is the route I walked, the map, the track and the photos.

::: tip
Start before 8:00. The chairlift queue after 9:30 in summer can easily take an hour, and the lakes are at their best in the morning light.
:::

::: curious
The names of the lakes describe their shapes: the Kidney, the Twin, the Eye and the Tear. The Eye is the deepest glacial lake in Bulgaria at 37.5 m.
:::

## What to take

| Item | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Windproof jacket | Even in August the wind on the Lake Peak is cold |
| 1.5 l of water | There are no springs above the Fish Lake |
| Cash | The chairlift does not always take cards |

::: warning
Do not swim in the lakes. They are a protected area and the water is a few degrees above freezing.
:::
